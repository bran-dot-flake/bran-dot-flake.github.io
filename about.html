PORTRAIT COMING SOON

Hi, I’m Brandon.

I’m an IT Specialist at Access Ohio, supporting Microsoft 365, Active Directory, endpoints, and networking across a multi-site organization in Columbus, Ohio.

I’m focused on growing into security operations and incident response. I enjoy tracing activity across logs and packet captures, building small tools, and documenting how I reached a conclusion. Longer term, I’d like to move into penetration testing.

My interest in technology started with game development and design. I’ve always enjoyed solving unusual problems and learning new technologies, and security gives me plenty of opportunities to do both.

Outside of work, I go climbing with friends, take long walks with my partner, and unwind with visual novels on my PS Vita.
